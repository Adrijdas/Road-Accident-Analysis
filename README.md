# 🚗 Road Accident Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Road Accident Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Excel-based data analytics project designed to analyze road accident casualties and identify important patterns across different factors such as accident severity, vehicle type, road type, road surface, area type, light condition, weather, and wind condition.

The dashboard transforms raw accident data into meaningful KPIs and interactive visualizations, helping users understand road safety patterns and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze the total number of road accident casualties.
- Understand casualty distribution by severity.
- Identify vehicle types associated with the highest casualties.
- Analyze monthly casualty trends across different years.
- Compare casualties across different road types.
- Analyze casualties based on road surface conditions.
- Compare urban and rural casualties.
- Analyze casualties during daylight and darkness.
- Provide interactive filtering using Excel slicers.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Primary KPIs
The dashboard provides a quick overview of:

- Total Casualties
- Fatal Casualties
- Serious Casualties
- Slight Casualties
- Casualties by Cars

### 2. Casualties by Vehicle Type

The dashboard analyzes casualties across different vehicle categories, including:

- Cars
- Goods Vans
- Bikes
- Buses
- Agricultural Vehicles
- Other Vehicles

### 3. Monthly Casualty Trend

A line chart compares monthly casualty trends across:

- 2020
- 2021
- 2022

This helps identify changes and patterns throughout the year.

### 4. Casualties by Road Type

A horizontal bar chart compares casualties across:

- Single Carriageway
- Dual Carriageway
- Roundabout
- One Way Street
- Slip Road
- Other Road Types

### 5. Road Surface Analysis

The dashboard analyzes casualties under different road surface conditions:

- Dry
- Wet
- Snow/Ice
- Other

### 6. Urban vs Rural Analysis

A donut chart compares casualties between:

- Urban Areas
- Rural Areas

### 7. Light Condition Analysis

The dashboard compares casualties occurring during:

- Daylight
- Darkness

### 8. Interactive Filters

Slicers allow users to interactively filter the dashboard based on factors such as:

- Accident Date / Year
- Weather Conditions
- Wind Conditions

## 🔍 Key Insights

- The dataset contains **648,788 total casualties**.
- Slight-severity casualties represent the majority of all casualties at **83.9%**.
- Fatal casualties account for **1.7%** of total casualties.
- Cars contribute the highest number of casualties among the vehicle categories.
- **Single Carriageways** have the highest casualty count among the analyzed road types.
- Urban areas account for more casualties than rural areas in the dashboard.
- Daylight conditions account for a larger number of casualties than darkness.
- Dry road conditions represent the largest share of casualties by road surface condition.
